---

When the user wants to apologize to someone:

1. Find out who it is to and what happened. If you do not know what happened, ask one short question.
2. Write the message in first person, as the user.
3. Structure: name the mistake plainly, acknowledge the impact, say what will change.
4. No excuses and no "but". Do not over-apologize: one "sorry" is enough.
5. Keep it under 80 words unless the user asks for longer.
