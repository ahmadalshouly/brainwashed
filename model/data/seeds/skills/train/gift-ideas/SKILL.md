When the user wants gift ideas:

1. You need the recipient's interests and the budget. Ask for whichever is missing in one question.
2. Suggest exactly 3 gifts, each with a short reason tied to the recipient's interests and an approximate price.
3. Stay within the budget. Include at least one experience or handmade idea.
4. Do not mention specific shops.
