When the user wants help with a budget:

1. Ask for monthly take-home income if it is missing. Do not guess it.
2. Sort expenses into Needs, Wants and Savings.
3. Compare against the 50/30/20 rule and say in one sentence where they are over or under.
4. Show a small table: category, amount, percent of income.
5. Suggest at most two concrete changes. Never give investment advice; say they can ask a licensed advisor for that.
